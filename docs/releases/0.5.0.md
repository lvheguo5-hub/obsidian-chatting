Chatting with AI 0.5.0 improves chat rendering, tool steps, mobile layout, model settings, and ChatGPT sign-in.

- Render common LaTeX delimiters and math fences as equations including fences nested under numbered lists, while preserving inline, fenced (including lists and blockquotes), and indented code. Wiki-link aliases work in tables with or without outer pipes.
- Follow new messages while at the bottom; keep your reading position when scrolling up, with a button to jump to the latest reply.
- Show compact tool steps with expandable parameters/results and clear failure states.
- Improve desktop panel spacing and phone keyboard/input layout.
- Populate thinking levels from provider model catalogs and respect models that do not support thinking.
- Add desktop browser sign-in with PKCE and automatic return. Device-code login remains available on desktop and is the current phone login method; mobile code-free login and credential transfer are not included.

Thanks to **Thomas Schierl (@thomas0schierl)** for PRs #20–#23. His original commits and authorship are preserved.

Validation: 217 automated tests; TypeScript, Svelte (zero errors/warnings), and production build; native desktop rendering/settings/chat QA; and physical iPhone Air ChatGPT conversations, production vault tools, and conversation/login continuity after a full app restart. The final rendering corrections were additionally checked in native desktop Obsidian. All 1,797 pre-existing visible phone test-vault files retained their hashes. API-key providers and a complete new desktop browser authorization were not retested with live accounts in this release cycle. Minimum Obsidian version remains 1.11.4.
